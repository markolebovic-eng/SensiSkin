# Evidence Ledger

**Internal strategy tool.** Tiers tell our team what is load-bearing, what to lead with, and how to answer a sharp client question. **Tiers never appear in a client deliverable.** The `Client-facing framing` column is what goes in a proposal or report.

**Tiers:** **A** official engine documentation · **B** measured study with stated methodology · **C** consistent independent practitioner reporting · **D** inference.

**Risk flags:** `none` · `policy-risk` · `requires-compliant-variant`. **This is the only column that gates a tactic.** Low confidence never excludes anything.

Every claim is scoped to an engine. An unscoped claim is a bug.
